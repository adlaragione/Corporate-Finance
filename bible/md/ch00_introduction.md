## Key ideas
- Five myths: valuation is not objective, not exact, not purely quantitative, not timeless, and more complex ≠ better (simpler models can do better on average).
- Asset-pricing questions nested: no-arbitrage (least general) ⊂ portfolio selection ⊂ equilibrium (most general). Business valuation is a general equilibrium pricing problem.
- Option view: equity = call on firm assets with strike K (= asset + put − riskless bond); risky debt = riskless bond − put.
- Cases: Ocado IPO (value depends on assumptions/perspective; price negotiated, value argued; valuation tested by new info; conditional on today's info), Facebook (scalability under uncertainty), Nvidia (P/E falls while market cap rises ⇒ earnings grew faster than price).
- Takeaway: valuation is a disciplined, transparent argument under uncertainty.
